Frequently asked questions

Product information and support.

What is Sortaroo?

Sortaroo is a photo and video organization application available for iPhone, macOS and Windows.

How does Sortaroo organize photos and videos on iPhone?

Sortaroo reviews media that is outside existing albums and helps you create albums by date in Apple Photos. You can optionally split same-day events, rename or merge albums, and search your organized albums.

Can I export iPhone albums to an external drive?

Yes. Sortaroo copies albums to storage available through iOS, including compatible external drives. The originals stay in your Photos library.

How does Sortaroo sort files on Mac and Windows?

Choose a folder and sort its photos, videos or both into folders by year, month or day. You can limit the sort to a date range and undo the last run.

What happens when Sortaroo finds duplicates?

On iPhone, Sortaroo flags possible duplicates for review and leaves deletion up to you. On Mac and Windows, exact copies are moved to a Duplicates area for review.

Sortaroo for Android is coming soon. Check back for availability updates.
